ABOUT / OUR FOUNDER

Vishal Shah

Founder & Managing Partner, MAAV Global

Vishal Shah founded MAAV Global to help organizations turn technology opportunity into commercial reality. He brings nearly three decades of experience building and scaling technology businesses. His work combines product strategy, strategic partnerships, and hands-on execution.

Previously, Vishal led Lenovo’s enterprise innovation and XR business. He has also held senior leadership roles at Qualcomm, Motorola, and Tech Mahindra. Across his career, he has led global businesses, built strategic partnerships, and launched products in more than 60 countries.

Vishal co-founded and successfully exited an AI/ML technology venture. He has advised Fortune 500 companies, venture capital firms, and technology startups on strategy, growth, and commercialization.

At MAAV Global, Vishal works with client teams and specialist partners to build products and develop routes to market. He also helps organizations apply AI to improve business performance. His focus is revenue growth and measurable business value.

Vishal’s speaking engagements include Mobile World Congress (MWC), CES, AWE, Lenovo Tech World, the Augmented Enterprise Summit, and Small Business Expo. His appearances span keynotes and industry discussions.

Vishal holds an MBA from Northwestern University’s Kellogg School of Management and an MS in Computer Science from Pennsylvania State University.
